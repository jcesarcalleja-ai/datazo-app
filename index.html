Datazo

Datazo is a short-video channel with one science curiosity a day in Spanish: what happens to your body, your brain and the world around you, explained in under 40 seconds.

The Datazo publisher is the desktop tool the channel owner uses to produce these videos and publish them to the owner's own TikTok account. It uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the account, the Content Posting API to upload and publish each finished video, and the Display API (video.list) to read the view counts of the account's own videos so the next video can build on what worked.

The tool only acts on the account that authorised it, never on other users' accounts.
