Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Datazo publisher ("the app") does with data from TikTok.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, used only to publish videos to the connected account.
  • Statistics of the connected account's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) via video.list.

How we use it

Only to publish the channel's videos and to decide which topics to cover next. We do not sell, share or transfer TikTok data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Tokens and statistics are stored locally on the operator's computer and are never uploaded to any server other than TikTok's own API.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is authorised. Revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) invalidates the tokens, and the local copies are deleted.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
